How it works

  1. Choose a lottery and your numbers. Pick them yourself or use quick pick. Add as many lines as you like and choose how many draws to enter.
  2. Pay from your wallet. You see the ticket cost and our service fee separately before you confirm. Orders close 3 hours before each draw so there is time to buy.
  3. We buy the official ticket. An agent in the lottery's home country buys a real ticket with your numbers and uploads a scan to your account.
  4. See the scan before the draw. If we could not buy your ticket in time, the ticket cost and the fee are refunded to your wallet automatically.
  5. We check the result. Winning tickets are confirmed against the official prize table and the full prize is credited to your wallet. We take nothing from winnings; local taxes on large prizes may be deducted by the lottery itself.

GlobeLotto is an independent ticket purchasing service and is not affiliated with any lottery operator.
