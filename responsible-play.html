Responsible play

Lottery play is gambling. The odds of winning a jackpot are extremely small, and no system or pattern improves them.

  • Only play with money you can afford to lose.
  • Set a daily deposit limit before you start.
  • If play stops being fun, take a break from your account.
  • GlobeLotto is for adults aged 18 or over only.

If gambling is causing you harm, free and confidential help is available from BeGambleAware and Gambling Therapy, which supports people in many countries and languages.
